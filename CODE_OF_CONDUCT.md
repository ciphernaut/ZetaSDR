# Code of Conduct

ZetaSDR follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the project's issues, gists and every other project space, and to anyone acting for
the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good faith, and keep criticism about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable.

To report a problem, use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting on the repository), and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are read
by the maintainer and kept confidential. The maintainer decides on any action, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Covenant.
