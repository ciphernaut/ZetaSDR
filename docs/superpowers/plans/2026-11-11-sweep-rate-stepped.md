# Plan 36.1: the stepped sweep, paced by the radio — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A `scan` steps any IQ source as fast as the source delivers settled samples (20 ms of them a step by default, no sleep, the FFT off the tune's path), at the width the source's capability gives, reports what a step costs, takes the operator's look (`dwell_ms`, `average`, `bits`), and the Soapy worker's ring carries `sc16`.

**Architecture:** The knowledge of when a tune has settled moves into each source (`Port::tune`'s contract), so the scan loop only waits on the node's ring for a dwell of samples at the new centre and hands the block to an FFT task through a one-slot channel. The grid's stride comes from `Capabilities.usable_fraction`, the capture depth from `Capabilities.depths` and `Port::set_depth`; the look lives in the scan's stored `fft` node beside its mask. The worker's ring becomes byte slots whose format (`cf32` or `sc16`) the worker writes into the header and names in the hello.

**Tech Stack:** Rust 1.97 workspace (tokio `watch` and `mpsc`, no new crates); `soapysdr` 0.5 (`Device::rx_stream_args`, `stream_args_info`, `hardware_key`) in the driver crate outside the workspace; vanilla JS page; the Playwright e2e suite.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-10-sweep-rate-design.md`, sections 3, 4.1-4.6a, and the 36.1 parts of 5, 6 and 7. Plan 36.2 (sections 4.7, 4.8) follows and consumes the "Produces for 36.2" lines below.

## The builder's calls (spec section 7)

- **Hand-off:** a `tokio::sync::mpsc::channel(1)` from the step loop to one FFT task in the job's `JoinSet`; a full channel is the loop's wait and is timed as `fft_wait_ms`.
- **The wait:** a per-source `watch::Sender<u64>` that `feed_ring` bumps after each push; the loop re-checks the ring on each change under a `tokio::time::timeout`.
- **One row a step:** through the registered `fft` stage (the backend seam), which gains `row_frames`; the scan does not compute spectra itself.
- **Averaging** lives in `zeta-core/src/spectrum.rs` (new), with the row quantisers moved there from `zeta-dsp/src/fft.rs` and re-exported, so 36.2's forwarded rows use the same ones.
- **`dwell_ms`, `average`, `bits` on a running scan:** `graph_put` on the stored `fft` node, beside `notch_hz` and `edge_mask`; the scan restarts as it does for the mask.
- **The scan moves** to `crates/zeta-core/src/node/scan.rs`.
- **"Taken after the return" in the Soapy reader:** `SoapyRx::read` gives counts, not timestamps, so the reader times its own `set_frequency` call and discards 1 ms reads until a read has had to wait for its samples (the driver's backlog is drained) and the settle has passed since the return, never more than `(call time + settle) × rate` plus one read.
- **The read length under a scan** is not shortened (smaller blocks would cost a ring slot each); the wait shows as `tune_ms` and a TODO entry carries it.
- **Device arguments:** `settle_ms=`, `usable=`, `bits=`. **Settle default** for an unmeasured driver: 10 ms.
- **The Kiwi's `SND`:** its header carries a frame count and no frequency, so the session drops frames for a delay (`tune_settle_ms`, default 250) after it sends a new centre.
- **A `--source-node` source** discards nothing: a tune starts a fresh remote stream whose frames carry the remote's own `center_hz`.

## Deviations from the spec, for the operator to confirm

- **Every depth is probed at open, the lossy ones too** (4.6a says a lossy depth is probed when first asked for). `Port::capabilities` returns `&Capabilities` and the worker port replaces its record once at most (`OnceLock`), so a record cannot gain a measurement mid-run. The cost is one more probe at the first open; the result is cached and passed back to respawns.
- **`max_bandwidth_hz` at the top level is the figure for the source's own depth**, fixed at open, not "the depth in force". `Port::depth()` and `set_depth`'s result name the depth in force and its cap; the ports bound a tune by it.
- **A ring of another `VERSION`** makes the child serve on the pipe, as its failed `Ring::open` does today (section 6's wording); a hello of another `PROTOCOL` or an unknown format is refused naming both (4.6's).

## Review Focus

1. **An upgrade over a cached source record.** `sources/<id>.json` written before this plan has no `usable_fraction` or `depths`; the supervisor keeps the cached record when a hello differs in more than the measured cap, so the live values would never apply. Task 4 (`a_cached_record_from_before_this_plan_takes_the_hellos_new_fields`).
2. **A dwell longer than the ring's history** (`dwell_ms` 5000 on a ring the budget sized to 1 s): the wait could never complete. The dwell is capped to the ring and reported. Task 7 (`a_dwell_longer_than_the_ring_is_capped_to_it`).
3. **A span inside one capture** (one step, no retune between steps): each step must take samples newer than the last, and the record must not be rewritten 50 times a second. Tasks 3 and 5 (`a_one_step_scan_takes_fresh_samples_each_step`, `the_timing_is_written_at_most_once_a_second`).
4. **Samples lost inside a dwell** (an overflow restarts the ring): the wait starts over within its timeout, and a source that keeps losing them is counted, not hung. Task 3 (`a_gap_inside_a_dwell_restarts_the_wait`, `a_port_that_stops_delivering_is_skipped_not_hung`).
5. **A scan at a lossy depth that fails or is stopped** while another request waits: no job is granted the source until it is back at its own depth. Task 8 (`stopping_the_scan_restores_the_depth_before_the_next_job_is_granted`, `a_failed_scan_also_restores_the_depth`).
